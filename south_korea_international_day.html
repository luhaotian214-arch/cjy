01 / The basics

A small country with a big story.

South Korea is in East Asia, on the southern part of the Korean Peninsula. Its capital, Seoul, is a lively centre of culture, technology and history.

The national flag

Meet the Taegeukgi.

South Korea’s flag is called the Taegeukgi. Its white background traditionally represents peace. The red-and-blue circle in the centre represents balance, while the four black trigrams represent ideas from nature and the universe.

The Korean writing system is called Hangeul. It was created in the 15th century under King Sejong to make reading and writing easier to learn.

02 / Culture

Tradition meets trend.

From beautiful traditional clothing to music enjoyed around the world, Korean culture has many different sides.

Tradition

Hanbok (한복)

Hanbok is traditional Korean clothing, known for graceful lines and vibrant colours. It is often worn for celebrations and special occasions.

Heritage

History & festivals

Palaces, folk music and holidays such as Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok help families celebrate Korean heritage.

Modern culture

K-pop & K-dramas

Korean pop music, television dramas, films and dance have built fans around the world and are part of the global Korean Wave, or Hallyu.

03 / Korean food

One table, many flavours.

Korean meals often bring together rice, soup and a variety of side dishes called banchan.

🥬 Kimchi (김치)

Fermented vegetables, most often napa cabbage or radish, seasoned with spices.

🍚 Bibimbap (비빔밥)

A colourful rice bowl topped with vegetables and other ingredients, mixed together before eating.

🔥 Bulgogi (불고기)

Thin slices of marinated meat, usually grilled or stir-fried.

🌶️ Tteokbokki (떡볶이)

Chewy rice cakes commonly served in a spicy-sweet sauce.

04 / Places to visit

Three places, three different moods.

South Korea has energetic cities, coastal views and remarkable natural landscapes.

Seoul 서울

Explore Gyeongbokgung Palace, busy markets, museums and modern city life.

Busan 부산

A coastal city known for beaches, seafood, colourful hillside neighbourhoods and its port.

Jeju Island 제주도

A volcanic island famous for Hallasan Mountain, lava tubes, waterfalls and beautiful coastlines.
